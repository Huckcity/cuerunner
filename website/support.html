Support

Write to orders@cuerunner.app. One person reads it — an order, a trial request, a licence, or something in the app that is not doing what it should.

Look here first

  • The troubleshooting reference: a cue that will not fire, a file that has moved, a device that dropped out mid-show, a trigger that is not being heard.
  • The guides build a show from nothing, and the reference has every cue type, field and setting.

A message that says what you did and what happened is answered in one pass rather than three, and a line from the log is worth more than a description of the sound it made.

Report a problem

Cuerunner ▾ → Report a Problem… builds the report for you and shows it in full before anything leaves: the version, the machine, what the audio engine says about itself, whether a key is active, the crash dumps Windows or macOS left, and the end of the session log. Say what happened in the box above it.

Copy and Save… keep it on your machine. Email… puts it on the clipboard and opens a message to orders@cuerunner.app for you to paste it into and send.

Cuerunner sends nothing itself, ever: no crash uploads, no usage counts. Your home folder is replaced by ~; the report holds no cue names, no show contents and no key. Crash dumps are listed by name and are not included — attach one if asked.

Keys

A key can be taken off a machine without asking anyone for permission. Settings… → License has a Deactivate button, and this is what pressing it does:

Deactivating puts this machine back to the free app. Your shows are untouched.

Activating a key, moving one to another machine, a key that has run out, a renewal, an invoice or a W-9: ask at orders@cuerunner.app. So is a refund. A refund is the row the key was sold under marked refunded, plus a request to deactivate the machine it was entered on. There is no window to catch.

Buying Pro, the prices and the trial are on the pricing page, which also walks through entering a key.
