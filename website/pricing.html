Pricing

The app is free to run a show: no key, no account, no expiry, on as many of your own machines as you like. Cuerunner Pro is one paid tier — the desk, the radios, the tablet and the second machine — and the whole of it can be tried first.

Try it for 14 days

Write to orders@cuerunner.app and say what you run and what you want to try. Say who the key is for: every key carries the name it is issued to, and the License tab reads it back to you. A person mints the key for you and it comes back by email. There is no checkout and no account in between.

  • Full Pro, 14 days. Everything a key turns on, so what you test is what you would buy.
  • Self-serve. Nothing to book: you write, the key is minted, and it arrives by email. No call, no card, no account first.
  • Offline. The key is checked on the machine it is entered on, against keys compiled into the app, so a venue machine with no internet unlocks like any other.

The 14 days run from the day the key is minted — the day it is sent to you — and not from the first time you open the app.

  1. Open Cuerunner ▾ → Settings… and choose the License tab.
  2. Paste the key into the field the tab offers for it — Paste the key from your purchase is what the empty box says — and press Activate.
  3. Checked on this machine, so a show computer never needs the internet. Nothing is sent anywhere when you press it, and there is no account behind it.

Enter it once and that is the whole of it: the key is checked on the machine it is entered on, there is nothing to install to receive it, and there is no account to sign into.

When a key ends

A trial is a key that lapses, and so is a rental: each one is good for the whole of its last day, in UTC.

When the last day passes, the machine is the free app again, with the show file untouched: the scenes, the mic plot and the triggers written during the trial stay in it, and the cue stack plays on.

A renewed key — a licence, a rental, another trial — brings the Pro half back on the spot, without a restart and with nothing to reinstall.

Buying it, and entering it

The key is emailed once the payment or the purchase order is settled — there is nothing to activate online and no account to make first. Enter it in Cuerunner ▾ → Settings… → License: it is checked on the machine it is entered on, and entering it tells nobody, so a venue machine with no internet unlocks exactly like any other.

One key covers two machines — the one you run from and the one standing by — and the free half keeps working whatever happens to it. The rest, including taking a key off a machine, is on the support page.
