Settings

Ctrl/⌘ + ,, or WinsLab ▾ → Settings. The sidebar splits them by where they are kept: this show travels in the .wlab file, this computer stays on the machine.

General

SettingDefaultWhat it does
Repeat guard300 msIgnores a second GO arriving this soon after the first, from the space bar, a trigger or a console. 0 turns it off.

Audio

SettingDefaultWhat it does
Audio systemthe platform's ownWhich audio system drives the show: the platform's own, or ASIO on Windows, which drives an interface through its own driver rather than the system mixer.
Output devicesystem defaultWhere the show plays.
Sample ratedevice defaultWhat the engine runs at. Files at other rates are resampled.
Bufferengine defaultFrames per block: lower is tighter, higher is safer.
Channelsdevice defaultHow many outputs the device offers the routing matrix.
ExclusiveoffKeeps other apps off the device, for every channel it has and steadier timing. A driver that refuses says why.
Trim silenceoffA default for new audio cues, not a switch over the show. Cues already in the list keep the trim they were made with.

Under them, what the engine is actually doing: backend, rate, device, latency, voices playing, media memory, and any dropouts. That block is a reading, not a setting.

MIDI

SettingDefaultWhat it does

Listen to MIDIProoffOpens the input ports you tick — all of them if you tick none — so cues can fire from notes and controllers.

NetworkPro

SettingDefaultWhat it does
OSC controlon, port 53000Takes cues and commands from consoles, desks and tablets. A port another program holds is reported rather than taken.
Find devicesoffLists gear that announces itself on the network — Dante endpoints among them — in the Monitor, and warns when one drops off.
↳ Service types_netaudio-arc._udp.local, _netaudio-cmc._udp.localUnder Advanced. What to browse for.
↳ Gone after30 sUnder Advanced. How long a device can go unseen before it counts as gone.
Tablet viewoff, port 53100Serves a read-only view of the show on the local network. An access code is required; anyone without it is refused.

Show Settings

WinsLab ▾ → Show Settings. These are the show's own, and travel in the file.

TabWhat is on it
ChannelsThe desk connection — OSC, MIDI or MIDI over TCP, with its address — the channel strips, and the DCA each rides. The line under the connection reads back exactly what will be sent.
CastPerformers, their parts, the channel each is on, and ensembles for opening a section at once.
Mic plotPacks, the channel each is patched to, and who is wearing it. Changing a performer on a pack records a swap.
